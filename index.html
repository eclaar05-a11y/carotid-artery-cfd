Project Overview

This project uses computational fluid dynamics to investigate pulsatile blood flow through a three-dimensional vascular model. The simulation examines the relationship between cardiac-cycle dynamics and important hemodynamic quantities, including pressure, velocity, and wall shear stress.

Objective

Visualize and analyze time-dependent cardiovascular flow.

Simulation

Three-dimensional CFD using SimVascular and svMultiPhysics.

Analysis

Pressure, flow, velocity, wall shear stress, and periodic convergence.

Discussion and Conclusions

The computational model provides a framework for examining pulsatile cardiovascular hemodynamics. The time-dependent pressure, velocity, and wall shear stress fields allow visualization of flow behavior throughout the cardiac cycle.

Cycle-to-cycle comparisons indicate periodic convergence of the simulated solution. Further analysis may investigate spatial variations in wall shear stress, flow distribution, and the influence of boundary conditions.
